Family Owned, Ozark Raised

Small town.
Strong coffee.
No filter.

Mean Muggin' opened on a stretch of Main Street across from the Houston post office, in a brick storefront that's watched this town go by for a lot longer than we've been pouring espresso in it. We're a locally owned family business — the kind where the person behind the counter remembers your order, and isn't afraid to give you a hard time about your "usual."

Our espresso is a locally roasted medium-dark blend, pulled fresh all day, every day. No syrupy shortcuts, no corporate script — just people in Texas County who take their coffee, and their customers, seriously. We may not have a drive-thru. We do have a reason to walk in.
